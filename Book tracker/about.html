📚 About BookVerse

Discover. Read. Track.

What is BookVerse?

BookVerse is a book discovery and reading tracker platform designed to help readers discover books and organize the books they want to read.

Users can search for books, save interesting books to their Favorites, and keep track of their reading progress.
